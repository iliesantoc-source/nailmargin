NailMargin Support

Need help with NailMargin? You can find answers to common questions below.

Account and Login

If you have trouble signing in, make sure you are using the same email address you used to create your NailMargin account. You can also use the “Forgot password?” option on the login screen to reset your password.

Appointments, Clients and Services

NailMargin lets you manage appointments, clients, services, pricing and business statistics from one place. Your data is linked to your account and can be synchronized securely.

NailMargin Pro

NailMargin Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID.

You can manage or cancel your subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased NailMargin Pro and it is not showing as active, use the restore purchases option available in the subscription screen.

Privacy

You can read the NailMargin Privacy Policy here: Privacy Policy

Contact Support

If you still need help, please contact us through the NailMargin website or the support contact provided in the app.
