Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NailMargin collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the NailMargin mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use NailMargin, we may process information such as:

  • Your email address and account information.
  • Your name, business or salon name, activity type, and optional phone number.
  • Information you enter about appointments, services, prices, expenses, and business activity.
  • Client information that you choose to enter into the application.
  • Subscription and purchase status.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and maintain your NailMargin account.
  • Provide appointment, client, service, and business management features.
  • Synchronize your information across supported devices.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Provide and verify NailMargin Pro subscription access.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the application.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

NailMargin uses third-party service providers where necessary to operate the application. These may include Supabase for authentication and cloud data services, Apple for App Store purchases and subscriptions, and RevenueCat for subscription management.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

4. Client Information

NailMargin allows users to store information relating to their own clients. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis or permission to enter and process their clients' personal information through NailMargin.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases made through the iOS application are processed by Apple. NailMargin does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

RevenueCat may be used to manage subscription status and determine whether NailMargin Pro features are available to your account.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate, secure, support, or provide the application's functionality, or when required by law.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as your account is active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

You may contact us to request deletion of your NailMargin account and associated personal data. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

NailMargin is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children.

11. International Users

NailMargin may be used internationally. Information may therefore be processed in countries other than the country in which you live, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy or data deletion request, please contact NailMargin through the support contact information provided in the application or on the NailMargin website.
