How do you calculate the price of a nail service?

Add materials and disposable supplies, the share of overheads allocated to one appointment, and compensation for your working time. You can add a reserve for business development. Use your own actual costs rather than copying another salon’s prices.

A worked pricing example

Materials 15 + allocated overheads 20 + 1.5 hours × 40 per hour = 95. A 10% reserve adds 9.50, bringing the estimate to 104.50. Express every amount in the same currency.

This example is illustrative. A business reserve is not net profit. Taxes, VAT and local obligations must be considered separately; they are not automatically included.

Why is revenue different from profit?

Revenue is the money received from clients. To assess your business results, subtract materials, rent, utilities and other expenses, including compensation for your work and applicable taxes. Also account for unpaid working time, cancellations and a realistic number of appointments.
