Why does gel lift near the cuticle?

Lifting can involve leftover cuticle, product touching the skin, moisture, unsuitable preparation or curing. Describe your products, application steps and when lifting starts so other technicians can offer relevant suggestions.

How do I choose the right nail products?

Discuss gel viscosity, base coat compatibility, lamp specifications and the condition of the natural nail. Share the exact products and techniques you use when asking for advice.

Open the community, write your question and choose a category. You can also answer existing questions and appreciate helpful replies.
